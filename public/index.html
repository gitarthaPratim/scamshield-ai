            📘 Cybersecurity Awareness

            The most common scams in India right now — and the one habit that stops almost all of them: pause, and verify through the official app or number yourself.

            🔑 OTP Scam

            Someone asks for the OTP sent to your phone "to verify" something. No bank or company ever needs your OTP — sharing it gives them your account.

            💸 UPI Fraud

            A "buyer" or "refund" sender asks you to approve a collect request or enter your UPI PIN to *receive* money. Receiving money never needs a PIN.

            🏦 Banking Scam

            Fake bank calls/SMS about account blocks, card expiry or "verification". Always check inside the official bank app instead of replying.

            💼 Fake Job Offer

            Easy work-from-home money for liking videos or simple tasks, after a small "registration fee". Real employers never charge a fee to hire you.

            🎁 Lottery Scam

            You "won" a lottery or lucky draw you never entered, but must pay tax/processing fee first. The prize does not exist — only the fee is real.

            🪪 KYC Scam

            "Update KYC or your account/wallet will be frozen today." KYC is done inside the bank branch or official app — never through a link in a message.

            📦 Fake Courier

            Your parcel is "held at customs" and needs a small COD/release payment on a link. Track parcels only on the courier's official website.

            🎣 Phishing

            Look-alike websites and emails that copy banks and big brands to steal passwords and card details. Check the exact domain spelling before typing anything.

            Do

            • Open your bank's official app yourself to check any alert.
            • Check the sender — real banks use IDs like VM-SBIINB, not random mobile numbers.
            • Call the bank / company on the number printed on your card or their official website.
            • Report fraud fast: call 1930 or visit cybercrime.gov.in.

            Don't

            • Never share OTP, PIN, CVV or passwords — no genuine organisation asks for them.
            • Never click shortened links (bit.ly etc.) in bank or government messages.
            • Never pay a small "fee" to release a prize, parcel or job.
            • Never act in a hurry because a message says "urgent" or "account will be blocked".

            Already clicked or paid?

            Do not panic — act fast: 1) Call 1930 (national cyber helpline) immediately. 2) Report at cybercrime.gov.in. 3) Call your bank and ask them to freeze the transaction. 4) Change your passwords from a safe device. Quick reporting in the first hours gives the best chance of getting money back.
